# Simple Interest Calculator

## Project Description
The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple Bash-based program that calculates simple interest using the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period provided by the user.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Input
- **Principal:** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate:** The annual rate of interest (percentage).
- **Time:** The duration in years.

## Example
For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
